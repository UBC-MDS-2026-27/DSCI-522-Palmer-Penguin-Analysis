# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Community

We aim to create a productive, positive, and inclusive team environment where everyone can contribute ideas, share knowledge, and collaborate effectively.

We value diversity in backgrounds, experiences, perspectives, and skills. We believe that different viewpoints strengthen our work and encourage better solutions. Every team member deserves to feel welcomed, respected, and comfortable participating in discussions.

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related activities, including in-person meetings, online discussions, emails, and collaborative platforms.

## 2. Our Principles

We strive to uphold the following principles throughout our collaboration:

### Be considerate

Our decisions and actions affect other team members. We should respect each other's time, responsibilities, and individual circumstances. We aim to communicate clearly, meet agreed deadlines, and consider how our actions may affect the rest of the team.

### Be respectful

Disagreements are a normal part of teamwork, but they are never an excuse for disrespectful behaviour. We should listen to different opinions, communicate politely, and address disagreements constructively. Personal attacks, insulting language, intimidation, and dismissive behaviour have no place in our team.

### Take responsibility for our words and actions

Everyone makes mistakes, and we should be willing to acknowledge and learn from them. When our words or actions negatively affect someone, we should listen to their concerns, take responsibility, and make a genuine effort to resolve the situation.

### Be collaborative

Our project is a shared effort, and its success depends on everyone's contributions. We encourage open communication, knowledge sharing, and active participation. We should support one another, distribute responsibilities fairly, and recognize that individual contributions are essential to the team's overall success.

### Be generous

We encourage team members to share knowledge, resources, feedback, and ideas. We should acknowledge each other's contributions and give credit where it is due. Helping others learn and overcome challenges benefits the entire team.

### Value clarity and constructive decision-making

Differences in opinions and approaches are expected. We should discuss disagreements openly, consider relevant information, and work toward decisions that serve the project's goals. When consensus cannot be reached, we should agree on a reasonable way forward rather than allowing disagreements to delay progress.

### Ask for help when unsure

Nobody is expected to know everything. We encourage team members to ask questions, communicate difficulties early, and seek help when needed. Questions should be welcomed, and everyone should make an effort to provide helpful and supportive responses.

### Step down considerately

If a team member is unable to fulfill an agreed responsibility or needs to step away from a task, they should communicate this as early as possible. They should provide relevant updates and documentation to minimize disruption to the rest of the team.

## 3. Leadership and Shared Responsibility

We believe leadership is a shared responsibility rather than a title or position. Every team member can take initiative, propose improvements, and help the team move forward.

We strive to:

- Take initiative when opportunities or problems arise.
- Make decisions transparently and communicate them clearly.
- Respect different contributions and recognize individual efforts.
- Support team members who take responsibility for specific tasks.
- Ensure that decisions consider the interests and workload of the entire team.

Leadership means helping the team succeed, not controlling others or dismissing their contributions.

## 4. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are inconsistent with our values and will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or intimidation.
- Insulting, humiliating, or personally attacking other team members.
- Using aggressive, offensive, or disrespectful language.
- Deliberately dismissing, undermining, or taking credit for another person's contributions.
- Repeatedly failing to fulfill responsibilities without communicating or making reasonable efforts to address the situation.
- Retaliating against anyone who raises a concern or reports inappropriate behaviour.

These expectations apply equally to every team member, regardless of their role, experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## 5. Reporting and Resolving Concerns

We encourage everyone to raise concerns when they experience or witness behaviour that conflicts with this Code of Conduct.

Whenever appropriate, concerns should first be communicated respectfully between the individuals involved. If the issue cannot be resolved, or if someone feels uncomfortable addressing it directly, they may bring the matter to the attention of the team or course instructor.

Depending on the severity and frequency of the behaviour, further action may include:

1. A discussion to clarify the concern and establish expectations for improvement.
2. A formal team discussion and documentation of recurring issues.
3. Escalation to the course instructor or relevant academic authority.
4. Further action in accordance with university policies and course requirements.

Serious incidents may be reported to the course instructor immediately.

All concerns should be taken seriously and handled fairly and respectfully. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is unacceptable.

## 6. Our Commitment

This Code of Conduct is intended to guide how we work together, not simply to establish a list of rules. We expect everyone to respect both its principles and its spirit.

We recognize that a successful project is measured not only by the quality of the final deliverables but also by how we treat one another throughout the process.

By participating in this project, we commit to creating a respectful, supportive, and collaborative environment where everyone has the opportunity to contribute and succeed.
